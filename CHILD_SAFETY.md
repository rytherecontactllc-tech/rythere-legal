# Child Safety Standards

RytHere is not designed for children and is intended for a general audience.

RytHere strictly prohibits content involving child sexual abuse or exploitation (CSAE).
Any content that exploits, endangers, or sexualizes minors is not allowed on the platform.

Users can report content or behavior that raises child safety concerns directly within the app using the reporting feature.
Reported content may be reviewed and removed, and accounts may be restricted as appropriate.

RytHere complies with all applicable child safety laws and cooperates with relevant regional and national authorities when required.

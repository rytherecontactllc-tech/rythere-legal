RytHere – Privacy Policy
Last updated: January 2026
RytHere (“we”, “our”, or “us”) operates the RytHere mobile application (the “App”). This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and handled when you use the App.
Our goal is to keep this policy clear, minimal, and aligned with how the App actually works.
________________________________________
1. Information We Collect
1.1 Account Information
If you create an account, we collect: - Email address - A unique account identifier
This information is used only to provide account access and basic app functionality.
1.2 Content You Provide
When you create a card, we store the information you choose to provide, such as: - Card title, description, and category - Photos or images you upload - Location details you manually enter (for example: city, ZIP/postal code) - Optional contact information you choose to include
Location data in RytHere is user-entered, not automatically tracked.
1.3 Usage Information
We may collect limited technical information necessary for the app to function reliably, such as: - App crashes or errors - Basic device and system information
We do not use analytics dashboards, behavioral profiling, or advertising trackers in the current version of the App.
________________________________________
2. How We Use Information
We use collected information only to: - Provide and maintain core app functionality - Allow members to create, view, save, and manage cards - Respond to support or report requests - Improve stability and reliability of the App
We do not sell personal data.
________________________________________
3. Payments
If donations or payments are enabled in the App: - All payments are processed by Stripe, a third-party payment provider - RytHere does not store full payment details such as credit card numbers - Stripe’s handling of payment information is governed by Stripe’s own privacy policy
________________________________________
4. Sharing of Information
We only share information when: - Required to operate core services (for example, hosting or payment processing) - Required by law - A member chooses to make information public by publishing a card
Public cards may be visible to other members and visitors.
________________________________________
5. Data Retention
We retain information only for as long as necessary to: - Provide the App’s services - Meet legal or operational requirements
Members may request deletion of their account and associated data by contacting support.
________________________________________
6. Your Choices
You may: - Use the App as a guest with limited functionality - Choose what information to include on your cards - Request account deletion by contacting support
________________________________________
7. Children’s Privacy
RytHere is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
________________________________________
8. Security
We take reasonable measures to protect information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
________________________________________
9. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date.
Continued use of the App after changes means acceptance of the updated policy.
________________________________________
10. Contact
If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at:
Email: rythere.support@rythere.com
________________________________________
End of Privacy Policy.
